How should I read this?

This model does not give a confident single diagnosis. Validation on held-out known interactions showed known, documented interaction triples average {{ baseline_known }} probability, while random, non-interacting pairs average {{ baseline_random }}. The badge above compares this pair's score against those two measured baselines, rather than an arbitrary cutoff. The ranked side effects are possible concerns, not a certain outcome — the model's top-ranked guess does not always match the documented side effect, even when it correctly flags a pair as elevated-risk overall.
