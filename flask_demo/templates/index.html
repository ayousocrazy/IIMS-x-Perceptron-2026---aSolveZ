How should I read this?

This model does not give a confident single diagnosis. Validation on held-out known interactions showed known, documented interaction triples average {{ baseline_known }} probability, while random, non-interacting pairs average {{ baseline_random }}. Each pair's badge compares its mean probability across all 1301 relations against those two baselines, rather than an arbitrary cutoff — severity reflects the overall interaction profile, not just whichever single relation happened to score highest. The ranked side effects are possible concerns, not a certain outcome — the model's top-ranked guess does not always match the documented side effect, even when it correctly flags a pair as elevated-risk overall. With more than two drugs, every pair is scored independently — the model does not model three-way or higher-order interactions.
